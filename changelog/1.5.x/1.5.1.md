# 1.5.1 — 2026-11-21

## Added

- **`pixoo_compose_scene` one-frame motion notice** ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/issues/66)). A scene that renders one frame and sets an `effect`, or an `animate` track of 2 or more keyframes, returns a `notice` naming each such element by index, type, and preset, marking those that draw nothing on that frame, and saying to set `frames`. The `effect` and `frames` descriptions, the design brief's `animation` and `troubleshooting` topics, and the design guide resource say the same.

## Changed

- **A reply at `PIXOO_IP` that is not a Pixoo's fails `device_invalid_response`** ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/issues/69)) on `pixoo_display_text`, `pixoo_compose_scene`, `pixoo_push_image`, `pixoo_render_html`, and `pixoo_overlay_text`: `ServiceUnavailable`, `retryable: false`, recovery naming `pixoo_discover_devices` and `PIXOO_IP`. An HTML page, an empty body, or JSON `null` failed `device_unreachable` (`retryable: true`) after four device requests, and JSON without a numeric `error_code` failed `device_rejected`; the call now makes two requests (one for the overlay) and retries none.
- **Rendering moves to `@cyanheads/pixoo-toolkit` 0.11** ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/issues/69)). An SVG icon fill lights a pixel when its centre is inside the shape, so filled icons shift by up to a pixel, and filled circles at some radii from 8 up lose the stray pixels outside their own outline. A still image with an EXIF orientation is drawn upright through `pixoo_push_image` and scene `image` elements; an animated WebP plays its frames as stored, as before.
- **Arrow heads.** The `arrow-left` head meets the shaft's tip at 16, 24, and 32 px, where v1.5.0 left a 1 px shaft nub past the point. At 12 px the `arrow-left` and `arrow-right` heads span three rows above the shaft and two below, where v1.5.0 drew four and three. At 12 px the `arrow-up` and `arrow-down` head rows are 1, 2, 4, and 6 px wide, so the head sits half a pixel left of the 1 px shaft.
- **Icon path nudges** keep `arrow-up`, `arrow-down`, and `arrow-right` identical to v1.5.0 at 16, 24, 32, and 40 px, the sizes where their head edges land on pixel centres; at most sizes between, the heads shift with the fill rule above. `pause` draws two 2 px bars at 12 px and 16 px.
- **Framework sync to `@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.22.** `engines.bun` rises to `>=1.4.4` (the Dockerfile and `packageManager` were already on Bun 1.4.4), and `createApp` refuses Node.js below 24, the floor `engines.node` already declares. `MCP_HTTP_ENDPOINT_PATH` is normalized (a leading `/` is added, whitespace trimmed), and `MCP_PUBLIC_URL` must be an `http` or `https` base URL with no query, fragment, or user info; a value with no single reading fails startup with a `ConfigurationError`. A server installed as a dependency and launched from the installing project's root resolves its application root to its own package, so `serverInfo`, the `skills/` folder behind `skill://pixoo-guide`, and the default `logs/` directory follow the server (`<project>/node_modules/@cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/logs`); set an absolute `LOGS_DIR` to keep logs in the project. In `scripts/`, `devcheck` exits 1 on an unknown flag or an `--only` value matching no check, `release:github` accepts `--help`, and the bundle cleaner and `lint:packaging` match dependency agent docs by the same positional rule that v1.5.0 carried locally, now including `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` at a package root. `tests/scripts/agent-doc-entry.test.ts` imports `lint-packaging` directly. The `framework-skills/` updates, `changelog/template.md`, the `.env.example` JWT audience and issuer lines, and the `scripts/check-framework-antipatterns.ts` header are doc or comment syncs.

## Fixed

- **A cancelled push stops sending device commands** ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/issues/69)). v1.5.0 returned the cancellation to the caller but still sent the device the rest of the push: a 10-frame push cancelled after its second frame sent the other 8 frames and the read-back, and a push cancelled while queued or waiting out the pacing interval still sent a channel read. Now none of those sends, a push cancelled during the channel read leaves the device on its channel, a cancelled push of more than 40 frames reports the cancellation rather than `gif_serve_failed`, and a push cut short this way still starts the pacing interval.
- **`BrowserRenderer.close()` waits for a browser that is already closing** ([#68](https://github.com/cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/issues/68)). A server shutdown during the idle browser's close could exit first and leave a `pixoo-browser-<pid>-XXXXXX` directory in the temp directory. The test fake browser now exits on `Browser.close`.
- **The two unit tests that bound port 8765 take a free port at run time** ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/pixoo-mcp-server/issues/65)); `tests/helpers/free-port.ts` holds the shared probe.
- **An image or sprite path, or an icon name, containing the words "Unknown color" fails with its own reason** (`asset_not_found`, `invalid_image`, `unknown_icon`), where v1.5.0 reported `invalid_color`.
